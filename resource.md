## 6. Quick Reviewer Path

1. Sign in as a recruiter and publish a role-specific challenge.
2. Sign in as a developer and submit a public repository; optionally complete the recorded session, repository quiz, and speaking round.
3. Sign in as two separate reviewers and submit independent rubric reviews for the credential.
4. Open the developer's CodePassport and recruiter Discover Talent profile to inspect verified evidence and available scores.

This path requires appropriately configured local accounts and provider keys. It is not a pre-seeded public demo.
